# Qualitative examples — base

## Example 1

**Model output**

```
**Symptoms:** Weakness in the left leg; daytime fatigue; occasional napping or snoring.
**Diagnosis:** High blood pressure, asthma, sleep apnea (cancer), colonoscopy, removal of benign polyps, removal of a benign lump from the breast.
**History of Patient:** High blood pressure, asthma, sleep apnea (cancer), previous stroke, colonic resection, removal of a benign lump from the breast, use of CPAP machine intermittently.
**Plan of Action:** Follow-up with Dr. X regarding sleep apnea, discuss the importance of adequate sleep and the potential risks associated with insufficient sleep and untreated sleep apnea.
```

**Reference**

```
Symptoms: Weakness in left leg, daytime fatigue, falling asleep at work
Diagnosis: Hypertension, asthma, sleep apnea, residual left leg weakness, obesity, hypercholesterolemia
History of Patient: Patient reports continued left leg weakness and improvement in motor functioning. She acknowledges falling asleep at work, which she attributes to looking at a computer screen. She experiences snoring and forgetfulness at work, with a supervisor offering her coffee. Patient receives 2-5 hours of sleep per night.
Plan of Action: Patient to follow up with Dr. X regarding sleep apnea treatment. Advised on risks of untreated sleep apnea, obesity, and hypercholesterolemia.
```
